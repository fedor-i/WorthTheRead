Worth Your Read

Checks whether an answer's claims are actually supported by the papers it cites — grounded in each paper's real results, with retractions flagged and overstatements caught. Runs entirely in your browser with your own Anthropic API key.

Your key

Stored only in your browser (localStorage). It's sent directly from your browser to Anthropic and to us never. The judge step needs it; link checks and retraction detection don't.

1 · Ask

Draft claims & find sources turns your question into a list of claims, each with up to two real papers discovered across OpenAlex, Crossref & Europe PMC in parallel, deduped by DOI and ranked by trust signals — citations, recency, open-access and evidence tier (needs your key). Each claim is checked against all its references, so a claim backed by several sources reads stronger than a single-source one. Then review and Verify. Or scope first with Clarify, or add claims by hand below. (Tip: prefix a search with !oa, !crossref, !epmc or !pubmed to scope it to one provider.)
