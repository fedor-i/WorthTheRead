Worth Your Read

Checks whether claims are actually supported by the papers they cite — grounded in each paper's real results, with every quote checked against the paper, retractions flagged and overstatements caught. Runs entirely in your browser with your own Anthropic API key.

Your key

Stored only in your browser (localStorage) and sent only from your browser to Anthropic. The judge needs it; link checks and retraction detection don't.

1 · Plan your search (optional)

Draft claims & find sources turns the question into 3–5 claims, each with up to two real papers found across OpenAlex, Crossref & Europe PMC (plus papers co-cited by the strongest hits), deduped, retractions dropped, ranked by trust signals. Clarify & plan scopes the search first. Or add claims by hand below. Tip: prefix a search with !oa, !crossref, !epmc or !pubmed to use one provider.

2 · Claims to verify

One claim per box, with the references it cites — edit anything before verifying.

3 · Verify

0.50

The loop runs on every claim: it looks for 2 more papers that support it and verifies the claim against each, and rewrites an OVERSTATED / UNSUPPORTED claim to match the evidence — re-verifying until enough independent sources support it or the rounds run out. Papers it finds are marked “🔎 found by search”; rewritten claims replace the originals above. (Needs your key; more rounds = more API calls.)

Audit a paper's citations

Paste a paper's DOI / PMID / PMCID. We pull its introduction and, for each sentence that cites a reference, check whether the paper it cites actually supports it, then show the introduction with every claim highlighted by verdict. Open-access papers in Europe PMC only (needs your key).

Fix a claim — find supporting sources

Paste a claim whose citation is weak, wrong, or missing. We search OpenAlex, Crossref & Europe PMC (two query angles, deduped) and verify the claim against each candidate, returning real papers that support it — so you can replace a bad citation, not just flag it. (Needs your key; it checks several papers.)
